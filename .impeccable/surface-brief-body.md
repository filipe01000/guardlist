# Application shell redesign

Mode: Operate. Scope: the complete authenticated product surface, including every current page, table, form, status, modal and responsive state.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The product is a controlled operations workbench. It refuses campaign composition, oversized metrics, slogans and decorative technical theater.

**OWN-WORLD:** Neutral charcoal workspace, one action blue, restrained semantic state colors, compact workhorse sans, tabular numerals only for data, thin rules, 8–12px radii and no decorative glow. Navigation and controls share one component vocabulary.

**STORY:** The operator sees current state, finds the next maintenance task, opens the relevant client, investigates or imports indicators and completes an export without losing client context.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A persistent 252px navigation rail frames a compact page heading, four-cell operational summary and the actual maintenance queue. The queue—not a hero—owns visual priority; search remains one action away.

**FORM:** Laboratory chain-of-custody worksheet, third grounded direction assigned by seed `c78796c8`; translated into a digital workbench rather than a literal paper effect.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
